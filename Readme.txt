FRESH MARKET - How to Use the Snap! Project
==============================================

This guide explains how to open and run the "Fresh market" project.
Fresh market is a mini online grocery (fresh vegetables) website built
inside Snap! It has a login system with two types of users:

   - ADMIN  -> already has an account, just log in.
   - MEMBER -> must register an account inside the project first.


STEP 1 - Open Snap! in your browser
-----------------------------------
1. Open Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
2. Go to this address and press Enter:

         https://snap.berkeley.edu/snap/snap.html

3. Wait a few seconds until the Snap! editor (the block area) appears.


STEP 2 - Load the "Fresh market" project
----------------------------------------
1. At the top-left menu, click  File  ->  Open...
2. Choose  Computer...  (or "Upload from your computer").
3. Select the file:   Fresh market (8).xml
4. Wait for the project to load. You will see the "Fresh market" stage
   and the sprites on the left.


STEP 3 - Start the project
---------------------------
1. Click the green flag  (the green triangle at the top-right of the stage).
2. The Fresh market login screen will appear.


STEP 4 - Log in
================

>>> A) IF YOU ARE THE ADMIN
--------------------------
On the login screen, type:

         Username : admin
         Password : 1234

Then click the "Login" button. You will enter the admin / admin area.


>>> B) IF YOU ARE A MEMBER (NORMAL USER)
---------------------------------------
Members do NOT have an account yet. You must register first:

1. On the login screen, click the "Sign Up" / "Register" / "Create Account"
   button.
2. Fill in the registration form:
         Username        : choose your own username
         Password        : choose your own password
         Confirm Password: type the same password again
         Gender          : male / female / others
         Address         : your address
         Email / E-mail  : your email address
         Contact Number  : your phone number
         Date of Birth   : your birth date
3. Click "Confirm" / "Next" to submit.
4. You are now registered. Go back to the login screen and log in with the
   username and password you just created.


STEP 5 - Using the Fresh market website
---------------------------------------
After logging in, you can:

   - Browse the list of vegetables (Pumpkin, Carrot, Long Bean,
     Lady Finger, Celery, Cucumber, Potato, Red Bean - each 1kg).
   - Click a vegetable to see its details, price and stock.
   - Click "Add to Cart" to put items in your cart.
   - Open your Cart and click "Pay Now" to check out
     (you will see "Payment Successful" and your order).
   - Click "Logout" when you are finished.
